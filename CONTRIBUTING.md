# Contributing

We welcome materials scientists, crystallographers, mineralogists, gemologists, bibliographers and responsible volunteers.

1. Open an issue with one clear question or deliverable. Use descriptive titles.
2. Include a stable primary citation for factual claims. Identify unverified statements explicitly.
3. For new experiment records, copy `experiments/TEMPLATE.md` and preserve raw data and uncertainties; scrub personal identifiers and confidential institutional data.
4. Ask reviewers to check scientific claims, unit conversions, safety, and whether results actually establish single-crystal kyawthuite.
5. Do not commit copyrighted full-text PDFs, confidential data, institutionally restricted details, credentials, or unverifiable synthesis procedures.
6. Proposed protocols must be vetted by qualified laboratories. Lab SOPs should be approved in the facility before any work.
7. Submit changes by PR where peer review materially improves a scientific claim. Small administrative/documentation corrections can be direct commits.
8. Contributors agree that their original text contributions are published under CC BY 4.0 and warrant they have rights to contribute them. External data may need separate terms.

We publish positive *and negative* results. Be precise, courteous, and generous with attribution.
