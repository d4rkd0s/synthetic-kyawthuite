# Project instructions for human and AI contributors

**Mission:** freely available, reproducible, scientifically honest research toward synthetic kyawthuite (BiSbO₄).

## Non-negotiable research rules
- Never invent experiments, laboratory partners, sources, results, measurements, or DOIs.
- Distinguish **primary result**, **secondary citation**, **interpretation**, **hypothesis**, and **proposal**.
- Never conflate stoichiometric BiSbO₄ powder with structurally verified single crystals or gem quality.
- Use original Kampf et al. (2017) mineral description as natural-phase reference, citing DOI:10.1180/minmag.2016.080.102; independently retrieve full historic synthesis papers before claiming an operational recipe.
- Do not turn a historical temperature range into instructions for pressurized synthesis. Require licensed/qualified laboratory safety and engineering approval.
- Preserve raw data, provenance, uncertainty, failures and calibration details. Do not silently improve results or omit contrary data.
- Respect copyright, licenses, institutional policies, safety, and chemical waste obligations.
- Document project work with descriptive GitHub issues and reference `owner/repo #number — title` in progress reports.
- Never mark a milestone complete without linked acceptance evidence.
- Prefer PR review for scientific data, procedures, and interpretation; routine project-maintenance changes may land on main.
- Avoid false claims of endorsement, partnership, funding or open-source licensing of third-party materials.
- Make documentation accessible and explain jargon.
