DOI: [10.1180/minmag.2016.080.102](https://doi.org/10.1180/minmag.2016.080.102). ## Evidence rules
1. Record DOI, title, author, pages, publication date, and stable URL before citing a primary experiment.
2. Clearly separate measurement, citation of historical measurement, hypothesis, modeling, and proposal.
3. No quotation of lengthy copyrighted material or wholesale PDF rehosting.
4. Do not infer hydrothermal pressure from temperature alone.
5. Update the evidence table when originals are acquired, including disagreements.
