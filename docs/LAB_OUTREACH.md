# Invitation to laboratories: open synthetic kyawthuite

## One-page collaboration brief

**Project:** Open Synthetic Kyawthuite
**Public repository:** https://github.com/d4rkd0s/synthetic-kyawthuite
**Material:** Bi³⁺Sb⁵⁺O₄, crystal structure corresponding to the mineral kyawthuite
**Goal:** Publish independently reproducible laboratory methods for crystalline BiSbO₄, ideally progressing to transparent/facetable synthetic single crystals, without licensing barriers on project-authored documentation.

## Established basis
Kampf et al. (2017), DOI:10.1180/minmag.2016.080.102, describes natural kyawthuite and cites historical synthetic BiSbO₄ growth by vapor, flux, and hydrothermal methods. The cited hydrothermal work reportedly yielded single crystals to 2 × 4 × 18 mm at 450–520°C. **The original full operational procedure is not yet verified in this project.**

## Requested response from laboratory
1. Relevant hydrothermal single-crystal growth, flux/vapor growth, and diffraction expertise.
2. Ability to locate and critically review historical growth literature, especially Popolitov (1996).
3. Facilities for qualified high-temperature/high-pressure work, engineering approvals, exposure control, and waste handling.
4. Available XRD, chemical/oxidation-state, Raman, and optical characterization.
5. Feasibility assessment, scope, expected deliverables, approximate budget, schedule, specimen ownership, publication/data-release terms, and any restrictions.
6. Whether a funded pilot, pro bono collaboration, or joint research proposal is feasible.

## Suggested phased engagement
**Stage A (desk study):** literature recovery, risk and facility assessment, draft laboratory-specific SOP, review gate.
**Stage B (pilot):** independently measured identity and at least one repeatable growth result.
**Stage C (optimization):** controlled growth trials, optical characterization, and publicly released results.

## Publication principles
Original project documentation is CC BY 4.0. Laboratories must explicitly agree to release applicable new reports, methods, and data; their pre-existing IP and any third-party publications are not automatically relicensed. Negative results are welcome. No confidentiality or endorsement is presumed.

## Safety
The repository does not provide unreviewed operating instructions for pressure autoclaves or hazardous antimony chemistry. All experiments are to be approved and carried out by suitably equipped and qualified laboratories.

## Response format
Institution/contact; methods/instrumentation; proposed study; key hazards and review process; estimated cost and schedule; open-publication terms; next decision needed.

**Status:** invitation template, not evidence that any laboratory has agreed to participate.
