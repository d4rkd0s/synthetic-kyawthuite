# Safety, risk assessment and responsible disclosure

**This repository is a research proposal, not an instruction to attempt crystal synthesis at home.**

Antimony-containing reagents/dust and heated processing can create toxic exposure hazards; the specific speciation, byproducts and waste hazards depend on the chosen chemistry. Hydrothermal crystal growth at elevated temperature and pressure can cause catastrophic equipment failure. Flux and vapor routes may introduce additional chemical and fume hazards.

Laboratory prerequisites:
- A trained principal investigator, institutional approval and lab-specific hazard/risk assessment.
- Documented chemical SDS review, compatible materials, engineered containment, exhaust/ventilation, PPE, environmental controls, spill and waste procedures.
- Certified pressure hardware operated strictly within the manufacturer/institutionally approved specifications; appropriate pressure and temperature monitoring and emergency plan.
- Site-approved SOP based on full primary literature and internal engineering review. Never extrapolate vessel pressure or load limits from the reported 450–520°C temperature range.
- No unreviewed pressure-vessel construction, open high-temperature handling of antimony compounds, or disposal to municipal drains.
- Record and escalate accidents, near-misses, and unexpected decomposition products before further experiments.

Do not publish personal information, proprietary facility details, or unsafe undocumented procedural guesses as if they were verified.
